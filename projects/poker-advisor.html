Preflop poker advisor

React, TypeScript, Express, PostgreSQL

GitHub

A Texas Hold'em strategy tool for the decision before the flop. Pick your two cards, your seat and stack, and what the players before you did, and it recommends fold, call or raise with a bet size, a confidence and its reasons. It works for tables of 2 to 9 players and saves hand history to Postgres.

How it works

The poker engine is written in TypeScript and runs in the browser, so the recommendation updates on every click. It places your hand in a starting-hand chart tier (premium, strong, good, marginal or weak) with a win rate and a playability score, groups your seat into early, middle, late or the blinds, and reads the action: how many players raised, how much it costs to call, and the pot odds. A decision tree combines those into the recommendation.

The hand strengths and win rates come from pre-built starting-hand charts rather than from simulating every hand, so the numbers are approximations. That was enough for what I built it for: learning the basics of preflop strategy.
